# WARAN Industries — Website Master Plan

North star: the site is a cinematic, scroll-driven headquarters for a long-horizon industrial organization that begins with WARAN PRIMAL and is architected for decades.

## Experience thesis

The visitor moves through expanding scale: human work → software → machine → biology → capital → Earth → orbit → beyond. Ambition is visual. Sequence is honest.

## Information architecture

- `/` cinematic headquarters
- `/divisions/[slug]` six unique division worlds
- `/frontier` exploration + space architectures
- `/about` institution and sequence
- `/research` labelled research directions
- `/contact` inquiry

## Engineering

Next.js 14, content modules under `/content`, 3D isolated to the homepage canvas, CSS/HTML fallbacks for reduced motion and missing WebGL, CSP headers, inquiry rate limit, sitemap/robots/JSON-LD.

## Careers Architecture (Google / Meta / OpenAI / Anthropic-Tier)

- `/careers` Frontier AI Enterprise Talent Framework covering 26 specialized functional divisions.
- Interactive Search Engine supporting natural language queries (e.g. "AI Product Roles 3-6 years", "DevOps 4-8 years").
- Multi-dimensional filters across experience bands, 26 disciplines, and 7 AI-Native Expectation Levels (`AI User` → `AI Systems Leader`).
- Detailed Job Specs with 30/90/180/365 Day Success Milestones, Team Collaboration Maps, Career Growth Pathways, and Candidate Signals evaluation matrix.
- Flagship 12-Month Rotational Industry Trainee & Future Leader Program with paid monthly stipend up to ₹1 Lakh/month.
- 10-step Candidate Application Platform producing unique application tracking tokens (`WAR-AI-2026-XXXXXX`).
